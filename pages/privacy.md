# Privacy policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

MoufCooks (cooking.mouftools.com) is a family recipe book. It is run by Enkefalos Holdings Ltd ("Enkefalos"), who are
responsible for your data under UK data protection law. Questions, or requests about your data:
[lemma@enkefalos.co.uk](mailto:lemma@enkefalos.co.uk).

## In short

- No sign-in, no accounts, no adverts, no tracking, no analytics, no cookies.
- The site never asks for your name, your email address or anything else about you.
- A few settings are kept in your own browser so the site remembers them. They never leave your device.

## What is kept in your browser

So that the site is as you left it, your browser keeps:

- whether you chose the light or the dark look;
- whether you chose the standard or the elevated version;
- the number of people you set for a recipe;
- the ingredients you have ticked off;
- the page you last reached in a recipe, for six hours, so you can pick up where you left off.

This is kept in your browser's own storage, not in cookies. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. To remove it, clear
this site's data in your browser's settings.

## What others receive when you visit

To show you a page, your device has to ask other computers for it, as it does for any website.

- **Vercel** hosts the site. It receives your IP address and details of your browser and device, and keeps logs so it can
  deliver the site and keep it secure. Vercel does this on our behalf. It is based in the United States. See
  [Vercel's privacy notice](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy).
- **Google Fonts** supplies the lettering. When a page loads, Google receives your IP address and details of your
  browser. Google says it sets no cookies for this. See
  [Google's note on fonts and privacy](https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy).

We do not add this to anything else, and we cannot tell who you are from it.

## Why, and on what basis

The only purpose is to show you the site and keep it safe and working. The lawful basis is legitimate interests: a
website cannot be delivered without it.

## How long it is kept

We keep nothing ourselves. Vercel and Google keep their logs for the periods set out in their own policies, linked
above.

## Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask for a copy of the data held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted,
and object to it being used or ask for its use to be limited. We hold nothing that identifies you, so there will usually
be nothing to give, but you are welcome to ask.

If you are unhappy with how your data has been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at
[ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/) or on 0303 123 1113.

## Changes

If the site changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page and the date at the top will change with it.
